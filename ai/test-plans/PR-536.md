## Intent check — what was corrupted, what the repair must restore

`#532` textually concatenated two versions of `run_provider_suites`, producing
an outer `for model, suite in run_list` loop wrapping an inner `for suite in
suites` loop that **unconditionally** printed "daily budget reached" and
`return`ed after the first iteration (a bogus hard-stop with no real budget
check), followed by a duplicated job body. It also left `run_provider_runs`
calling `select_models_within_budget` (never imported) and `budget_file`
(sibling-scope only) → two F821 NameError crashes + a dead `budget` var
(F841). Separately, a merge dropped the #508 `TestAgenticDatasetIsCurrent`
class while its import stayed live → F401 in `tests/test_bootstrap_dashboards.py`.
